2. Service Description & Digital Nature of Platform

Rapid PVC is a cloud-based software utility designed specifically for Common Service Centres (CSC), Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLE), Cyber Cafe operators, and digital printing businesses across India.

  • Layout Automation: The platform processes user-uploaded official PDF documents (such as e-Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, and Ayushman cards) and reformats them into CR-80 standard PVC card dimensions (85.6mm × 53.98mm) at 300 DPI high-resolution for instant printing on thermal, inkjet, and laser card printers.
  • Formatting Utility Only: Rapid PVC does NOT issue, modify, fabricate, verify, or register government identification credentials. The software functions strictly as a document layout and alignment formatter.
  • Instant Delivery: Services are rendered automatically and instantaneously upon operator request. Once a print layout is generated, the digital output is immediately ready for download.
