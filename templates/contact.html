Frequently Asked Support Questions

Quick solutions to common questions before reaching out.

💳 Amount debited but wallet not credited?

Banking payment gateways occasionally take 2-5 minutes to confirm settlement. If not updated within 15 minutes, email us your UPI/Card UTR or Transaction ID and we will manually reconcile or refund it.

📄 Card generation failed or corrupted?

If your wallet balance was deducted but a technical glitch prevented file download, the deducted credit will be restored to your account or refunded as per our Refund Policy.

⏳ Does my wallet recharge balance expire?

No. All recharge packs on Rapid PVC come with 100% Lifetime Validity. Your unutilized balance remains in your account indefinitely until used.
