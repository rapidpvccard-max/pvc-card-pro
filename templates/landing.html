Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PVC card printing with Rapid PVC.

1. How do I print the card on my printer?
Download the A4 Print File (PDF), open it in your browser or Adobe Reader, and select 100% Scale / Actual Size in your printer settings. Print on glossy photo paper or 4x6 / A4 sheet.
2. What is the cost per card?
Cards cost only ₹0.95 per card with our ₹100, ₹200, and ₹300 packs. The ₹20 Trial pack is available for beginners at ₹2.00 per card.
3. Does my wallet balance ever expire?
No. All recharges come with 100% Lifetime Validity. Your balance stays in your account until you use it.
4. How does password unlocking work for Aadhaar PDFs?
Simply enter the citizen's password in the password box. For Aadhaar, the standard password is the first 4 letters of the name in CAPITAL + year of birth (e.g. SURE1995).
